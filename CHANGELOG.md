# Changelog

## 0.1.0 — 2026-10-29

- Three connected investigations of Gaius's ownership rules: an owner's
  informal delivery, an innocent buyer's purchase from a non-owner, and land
  entered peacefully or by force.
- Separate possession, civil title and the in bonis position of 2.41; whole-year
  progression and pinned comparisons that identify changed facts.
- Latin passages, close English, contextual phrase notes and complete pages
  from the 1904 Poste–Whittuck edition, with visible editorial boundaries.
- Editable case files, explicit shared links, SVG diagrams and a standalone
  illustrated casebook with native downloads and printable sources.
- Integrity-checked sources and offline edition installation, with source-cache
  recovery and protection against late page responses.
