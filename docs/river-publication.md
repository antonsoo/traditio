# River histories: public delivery and audit

[Open the river bench](https://antonsoo.github.io/traditio/river.html#river-bench) or
[find it through its Latin](https://antonsoo.github.io/officina/?q=ex+eo+tempore).

The new lesson follows a carried parcel, imperceptible accretion, a planted tree
and an inundated field through histories. It retains eight Latin readings,
close English, 23 contextual phrase notes and four complete printed pages.
A learner can preserve an original ending and try a different continuation:

```text
Arrival: Lucius
  + receiving-soil roots + long attachment -> Aulus  (original ending)
  + long attachment, without new roots    -> Lucius (new continuation)
```

The comparison identifies the differing physical fact under the same selected
passages. Selecting earlier points does not backdate acquisition. Selecting the
shorter Institutes passage can leave later attachment unresolved; it is not a
switch between historical dates of a supposed legal change.

Offline verification passes the original site's TLS bytes through CONNECT,
closes its existing tunnels and refuses new connections. A fresh browser context
without a service worker must fail to reach the origin. Already installed
lessons then reload and export the same illustrated readings. The audit also
damages a saved source and verifies that both open lessons withdraw readiness;
connected recovery rechecks the files and preserves the editable explanation.
Firefox's emulated-offline source read can use valid HTTP-cached bytes, so that
observation is recorded separately from actual transport refusal.

The original source CI and Pages jobs for the recorded source revision concluded
without executing any steps. They are not counted as successful CI. GitHub's
legacy Pages deployment for the publication branch did complete; the canonical
bytes and real browser behavior were verified independently.

The new translations and legal-historical interpretations have not had independent
expert review. These are bounded applications of selected clauses to stipulated
facts. The tagged river PDF has 26 pages and no extracted word outside the checked
35-point inset; no physical-printer test or general PDF accessibility certification
is claimed.
